# 有你的橘_橘色科技不是橘色柯基

**Live Demo:** https://wp1141-weld.vercel.app/

![Homepage](images/homepage.png)

A first-person perspective online exhibition that transcends the boundaries of virtual space—this is not merely a virtual gallery, but a living blueprint that can be brought into a real apartment.

Built through a creative pipeline: hand-drawn 2D sketches → Sweet Home 3D for walls and interior design → Blender for material definition → JavaScript/CSS for web construction, resulting in an immersive online micro-exhibition.

## Design Philosophy

### Reality and Contrast

Two distinctly styled yet equally comfortable rooms share the exact same real apartment layout. What we aim to convey is not that orange technology is the sole solution, but rather, through this experiment, we hope to discover together: when 'people' become the center of design, how can subtle spatial changes and technological assistance create life experiences fundamentally different from past encounters?

### Transition and Reflection

Between exhibition spaces lies a transitional area simulating an entryway. Please pause here, shift your mindset, and prepare to move from the cold reality into warm solutions.

### Listening and Contemplation

As a visitor, wander through these living scenes. Seek out the gently glowing objects—these are the clues we've left behind. Upon clicking, you'll discover through text or family voice dialogues what challenges lie hidden within these seemingly ordinary gaps in daily life.
